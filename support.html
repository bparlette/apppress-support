Frequently asked questions

The app isn't working — what should I do?

Try these steps in order:

  1. Force-quit the app and reopen it (swipe up from the bottom of the screen, then swipe the app away).
  2. Restart your iPhone.
  3. Check that you're on the latest version — open the App Store, tap your profile icon, and pull down to refresh, then update the app if an update is available.
  4. If the problem persists, delete and reinstall the app. Note: because our apps store everything on your device, reinstalling will remove your on-device data. Export or back up anything important first if the app offers an export option.

If none of these help, contact us via the App Store with your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Pro upgrades are one-time purchases tied to your Apple ID. To restore:

  1. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.
  2. Open the app and look for a "Restore Purchases" option (usually in Settings).
  3. If there is no restore button, deleting and reinstalling the app from the App Store with the same Apple ID will restore the purchase automatically.

Apple handles all payments — we never see your payment information. If you're charged but don't have access, check your receipt in the App Store (profile → Purchase History) and contact Apple Support for billing issues.

Is my data private?

Yes. All of our apps work 100% offline. Anything you create in the app stays on your device — it's never sent to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Read the full Privacy Policy.
