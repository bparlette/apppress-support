Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all apps published by AppPress, including but not limited to PunchListPDF, GeoStamp, JustTheRecipe, NagMe, ScanLocal, StreakGrid, GutSafe, DeckMasterFSRS, SurgeCalm, HydroTap, PulseFloor, FirstBites, CrownSwipe, JokerHands, BorderStamp, DiceDungeon, SpireCrawl, FocusGrove, NextActionGTD, CalcPadLive, MetroFlow, GridAgent, LogicMachineVM, IllusionValley, MarginMarkPDF, and OutbreakSEIR (collectively, "the Apps").

The short version

Our apps work entirely on your device. They do not send any of your data to us or to anyone else. There is nothing to collect, store, or share — because the apps never connect to our servers at all.

Data collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Specifically:

  • No accounts. The Apps do not require you to sign up or sign in.
  • No analytics. The Apps do not include any analytics, crash-reporting, or telemetry frameworks.
  • No tracking. The Apps do not track you across apps or websites, and do not use any advertising identifiers.
  • No connection to us. The Apps never send data to our servers. Most work fully offline. JustTheRecipe connects to the internet only when you ask it to open a recipe link: it downloads that one web page directly from the recipe website, and nothing about you is sent to us.

Data stored on your device

Any content you create in the Apps (for example, punch lists, stamped photos, recipes, or notes) is stored only on your device, using Apple's standard on-device storage. We never see it, and it is never transmitted to us or any third party. If you delete an app, its on-device data is deleted with it.

Permissions

Some Apps may request device permissions — such as camera or photo library access — in order to perform their core function (for example, stamping a photo with GPS coordinates). These permissions are used only on-device for the feature you requested. You can change or revoke permissions at any time in your device's Settings app.

In-app purchases

Some Apps offer a one-time "Pro" upgrade as an in-app purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, process, or store any payment information.

Children

The Apps do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us via the App Store listing for any of our apps.
